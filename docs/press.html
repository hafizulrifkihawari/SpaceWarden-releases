Short description

SpaceWarden is a free Mac app that shows what fills your disk and helps you free up space in a few clicks. Everything goes to the Trash first, and your files never leave your Mac.

Long description

SpaceWarden maps your Mac in seconds with a storage map, simple categories and the biggest folders. Recommended actions show how much space each one frees and how safe it is. It finds large files that you have not opened in months, exact duplicates, browser and developer caches, and apps you seldom use with their leftover files. A Media Library shows all your photos, videos, music and documents on one screen, and quick previews let you look inside files before you remove them.

Every default action moves items to the Trash, and the Activity screen can undo each move, rename and trash. SpaceWarden works offline. It has no account and no tracking. It never scans passwords, keys or private messages, and it goes online only when the user selects "Check for Updates".
